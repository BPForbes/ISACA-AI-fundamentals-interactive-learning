Learning snapshot

AI foundations

AI is the broad field of systems that perform tasks that would typically require human intelligence.

Machine learning

Models learn patterns from data and improve as they are trained and evaluated over time.

Responsible use

Governance, privacy, fairness, and accountability are essential to safe and trustworthy AI adoption.
